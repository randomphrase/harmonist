{# What to do about an update other than take it (#271), inside the update section it belongs to (#366). A CHECKBOX, not two buttons. It was `Ignore for now` becoming `Stop ignoring` — which shows the ACTION and leaves the state to be inferred backwards from it. That is the argument #227 already made when the incomplete-album acceptance stopped being a button and became a checkbox beside the badge it governs, and it applies here word for word. A checkbox is simply the native control for a persisted boolean (web-ui rule 1), and `change` is its default trigger, so there is no `hx-trigger` to get wrong (rule 2b). `name`/`value` are the platform's own contract too: a ticked box posts ignore=true, an unticked one posts nothing, and the endpoint's default reads that as taking the ignore back — the same shape as `/tracks-unavailable`, and the reason the two endpoints this used to need collapsed into one. THE LABEL CARRIES THE PROMISE, not just the state. "Ignored" alone reads as *silenced*, and someone who believed that would have no reason ever to tick it: what makes this safe to offer is that the album comes back by itself when MusicBrainz next moves the release, so the edit you go off to make is exactly what returns it. Hence "until MusicBrainz changes" in the label, spelled out in the title. See the 7->8 migration in `activity_store` for why the refusal version could not be built. The upstream link is the other answer, and the one that actually fixes anything. MusicBrainz is canonical (docs/design.md §1) — Harmonist applies what it says and keeps no local exceptions — so "this update is wrong" is not a fact about the album, it is MusicBrainz being wrong, and the fix belongs there, where it benefits everyone and comes back on the next check for free. `oob` is set by the endpoint that toggles this and unset when the section includes it: the section is itself an out-of-band swap, and nesting one inside another would have htmx process the same block twice. #}
