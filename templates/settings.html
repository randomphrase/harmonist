Tag transforms

Named, exactly-checkable reshapings of what Harmonist writes — the ones Picard does through its options and tagger scripts. These change what a future tagging writes: albums already on disk aren’t rewritten to pick one up, they gain it the next time something else about them changes. Both spellings count as correct on disk either way, so turning one on never marks your library as having updates to take.
